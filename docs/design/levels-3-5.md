# Sectors 3 to 5: ten ideas, three picks

2026-10-26. Albert: "First two levels are fine. Let's try to get 3 more that are actually fun and only require two
instructions." The reader holds two cards, so every idea below has a two-card answer, and the naive decks a new
operator would try first have to fail in a way the scope and the tape make readable.

What a good sector needs here:

- **One idea**, discovered rather than told: the "oh!" when the second card goes in.
- **The obvious decks fail visibly**, and each failure points at the idea (the tape says why).
- **Different from the sectors before it.** The Dark: the beacon isn't a path, follow a wall. The Jellyfish: light
  attracts, go dark (or burn them).
- **No second solution that dodges the idea** (the exploit hunt checks every one-card deck and most two-card ones).
- It pushes the story on: the Company, Op. 5, and whatever is down there.

## The three

**3 · The Listeners** (STOP). **4 · The Gallery** (KEEP DISTANCE). **5 · The Lights** (SWIM TOWARDS LIGHT).

Three answers to the deep: *hold still*, *back away*, *follow*. Each sector issues exactly one new type plate, so the
new card on the wheel is the hint, and the previous sector's answer is the trap (stopping kills you in the Gallery;
in the Lights, the beacon and the wall both lie). The Nursery, the Valve and the Lock stay in the game for later,
when the hangar opens more slots.

Results (survey fleet, 20 dives each; full tables in rooms.md):

| Sector | Intended | Naive decks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Listeners | 20/20 | beacon 4/20, stop at 2 tiles 1/20, keep distance 6/20, lamp off 4/20, laser 0/20 |
| The Gallery | 20/20 (keep distance 6 too) | beacon 0/20, stop when near 0/20, laser 0/20, follow a wall 0/20 |
| The Lights | 20/20 | beacon 0/20, follow a wall (either hand) 0/20, the lights alone 0/20, fixed routes 0/20 |

Story: the eels are the first thing that *listens*; Op. 5 notices the last words make one sentence; the lights are
not on the Company's chart ("There are no lights in Sector 5. Operators reporting lights will be referred to
Welfare"), and they go out behind Robbie "like somebody walking him home".
