# Albert's playtest notes, 2026-10-17 (verbatim, numbered as he sent them)

1. The core progression mechanic is not clear. I want to know how to get more CPU. How to purchase new instructions,
   and also new instruments or equipment. How to earn "money" or whatever is spent to get those things. In my mind,
   there should be two separate currencies, one for equipment and one for instructions. They should be obtained in
   different ways. I think we should brainstorm how to get each one. There should therefore be a shop or workshop to
   purchase those things.
2. The game screen should go into full screen when you click to send a Robbie. Pressing Spacebar or the keyboard keys
   of left / right should let you toggle the speed of the replay, including x0,5. There should also be an option that
   you can toggle on-off, for speeding until a bit before the place where you died last time, and then going to your
   currently selected speed.
3. The game badly needs a tutorial.
4. It was really hard for me to discover the button for "Send fleet". I think it should be big and obvious. Anyway, we
   really need a tutorial.
5. You should be able to go to the next level as soon as you reach the beacon. BUT there should be some "extra" beacons
   or extra missions for the level, that give you currency to upgrade. So you can just aim to reach the beacon, or you
   can aim to complete all/some of the objectives and then get more points to upgrade.
6. Three screens for now: "Game", "Mission Control", and "Lab". Mission Control is what we currently have. The Game is
   when we click launch Robbie, and it goes full screen. The Lab is where we upgrade: a cute sprite of our current
   Robbie in big size; you see when you change a component or equipment. Robbie has limited slots, so you choose
   between purchased / discovered components for each mission. Cute, steampunky, think Outer Wilds but 2D and more
   cartoonish (drawn, not 3D).
7. Mission Control and Game need to be better too. Right now it feels like a browser game: all text and screens except
   the game. The graphics are too simple. The mechanics and top-down perspective are good. We need better graphics.
8. CPU budget is currently too tight. We need more CPU clearly, or a way to increase it fast.
9. Everything should be more cute.
10. Love the messages from Robbie. Every time he dies he should send a personalised message, chosen according to how
    he died. We should write hundreds of them so they rarely repeat. Reading them is a little reward.
11. EVERYTHING should be invisible unless you discover it with your lantern. That includes beacons and enemies.
12. Good level design!
13. Walls that move, everything that moves. The design should be way more dynamic and cute. It can't all be blocky.
14. I only made it to level 4. Something's broken there, I can't get past the lurker no matter what. Robbie simply
    doesn't see him.
