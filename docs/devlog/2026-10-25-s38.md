# Session 38 · 2026-10-25 · Two views, red for hands, the drums back

Albert played 0.2.0 and looked at the three art mock-ups. He liked **A (3D) or B (lived-in 2D)** and asked for a
testable version of each, plus:

- **Red on everything touchable**, so players know instinctively what they can work (wheel rims where you turn them,
  knobs, levers).
- **The post-its are weird** and fall down weirdly; find something better.
- **The note-destroying animation** (the shrinking card going into the pulp chute) feels wrong.
- **The wheels are good, but showing only the current choice makes choosing hard**: bring back the drum behind glass
  showing the previous and next entries.

## What changed

- **Two views in one build** (`console/operator.gd`, the new root). A, from the chair (`console/chair.gd`): the
  console renders into a SubViewport whose picture is laid on two faces, the upright panel and the press/teleprinter
  sloping out at 42°, in a dark room: cabinet cheeks and hood, a desk with papers, a mug and a pencil (yellow: not part
  of the machine), pipes on the bulkhead, a lamp swaying on its cable with a shadowed spot, green spill from the scope,
  dust in the light. Touches are ray-cast onto the two faces and pushed into the viewport as mouse events at console
  coordinates; pinch (or wheel) leans the camera towards a point; memos and the Wall come up flat. B is the console
  as before. Switch at the top left or V; kept in `user://view.cfg`.
- **The weathered desk is now the plate** (console_scene.py `grimy`, then weather.py, which now keeps the brass name
  plaques clean). The sprites are rendered grimy too.
- **Red means hands.** New `red_enamel`/`red_bakelite` materials on wheel rims and grips, knob bodies, recorder key
  tops, the NOT toggle's tip, the crank grip, the capsule's bands, the lid handle, reel hubs, platen knobs, the gain
  knob, the MAGNIFY bed, a ribbon on the binder; cards carry a red band. Op. 5's welcome note says it.
- **Drums behind glass** in place of the split-flap (deleted): `Drum` gained `wrap` (a full drum, the last entry above
  the first) and `shade`; the wheels roll the drums and a drum rolled by hand turns its wheel (`Wheel.turn_to`). Layout:
  `drum_window`, `order_drum`, `cond_drum`; the tray moved down 12 px.
- **Op. 5 writes in grease pencil** (`note.gd` rewritten): letter by letter on the reader's glass, the scope's glass or
  the empty tray, a loop round each thing he means (`Vector3` targets: position and loop size) and an arrow to the
  nearest; wiped off with a smear when done. Notes ignore input. Notes on the reader stack under the cards in use.
- **Pulping**: the card is crumpled into a lumpy paper ball (faceted polygon pulled in unevenly), tossed underarm into
  the chute, sinks darkening into the throat, the flap rattles while the cutters chew, shreds spit back out.
- `tools/console_check.sh` runs the tour and the 33-check touch play-through in both views; all pass.
