# Block 3 — 2026-10-17, 01:00 UTC: a fresh swarm on every dive

## What the exploit hunt found

The Jellyfish hunt turned up programs that never look at the water and still pass, for example

```text
move 45 if energy_below 75
move 135
```

(4 CPU, 20/20). It swims north-east until the energy meter drops, then turns south-east, and the route
happened to thread between the jellyfish. They started in the same 20 places on every dive, wandering only
a little, so a lucky fixed route was a real solution. That breaks the room's promise: the lesson is
"light attracts", and a player who found this would have learned nothing about the jellyfish.

## The fix: the swarm moves

1. **Scattered swarm.** Twelve of the hand-placed jellyfish in the middle of the room are gone. Eight
   still rest along the rock (they keep `follow_wall` honest). Sixteen more are now scattered over open
   water on every dive, drawn from the dive's own random seed, so dives stay deterministic and replays
   stay exact. A scattered jellyfish never starts within 6 tiles of Robbie, 3 of the exit or 2 of another.
2. **Lamp glow.** A lit lamp used to attract only jellyfish in its beam. It now also attracts any jellyfish
   within 3 tiles all around Robbie (`LAMP_HALO`, 48 px). Without that, the fixed routes survived by
   keeping the swarm behind them, where the beam never pointed.

I tried a smaller scatter (12) first; the fixed routes still got through sometimes, so it went up to 16 and
gained the glow. Measured with 20-seed fleets after the change:

| Program | Survey fleet (20 seeds) |
| --- | --- |
| intended, dark (`lamp off` / `keep_distance 2` / `move_towards beacon`) | 18/20 |
| intended, laser | 20/20 |
| the fixed route above (was 20/20) | 0/20 |
| a zigzag route (`move 45 if not wall_ahead` / `follow_wall left`) | 0/20 |
| `move_towards beacon`, lamp on with keep_distance, wall hug | 0/20 each |

The fixed route is kept as a reference program (`jellyfish/naive_clock.rbt`), and must fail the survey fleet.

![Three dives of the intended program, and the fixed route](img/s05/jellyfish-swarms.png)

## A new weakness, noted rather than fixed

The intended dark program now loses 2 dives in 20, both the same way: a jellyfish drifts towards Robbie, Robbie
backs away facing it, and backs into rock. `keep_distance` reverses blind. The real survey seeds (101–110) all
pass, so certification is not at risk, and the failure reads well in a replay ("I backed into the wall").
It may become a lesson of its own later (a `wall_behind` condition, or a room built around it). In the backlog.
