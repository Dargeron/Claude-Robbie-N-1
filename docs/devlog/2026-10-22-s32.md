# Block 30 — 2026-10-22, 03:29 UTC: a progression proposal for Albert (#1, #5, #6, #8)

## Why

The biggest items on Albert's list wait on one decision of his: the two currencies. Rather than wait idle, this block
writes the whole progression down as one proposal he can approve in a line, so the build can start the moment he
answers.

## What changed

- **docs/design/progression.md**, the proposal:
  - Reaching the exit opens the next room; the survey becomes an optional goal.
  - **Salvage**, from caches in rooms and the wrecks of earlier Robbies, buys equipment and slots in the Lab.
  - **Data**, from charting, first sightings, surveys, lean programs and first losses to something new, buys
    instructions and CPU.
  - Every room goal pays once, with a table of amounts. The six rooms pay about 400 Data and 150 Salvage in all.
  - Instructions a room teaches come free; extras are bought. CPU modules add +2 to every room's budget, in three
    levels.
  - Equipment ideas that change puzzles rather than numbers: dim lamp, spare battery, hull plate, sonar ping (shows
    lurkers), soft fins (quieter for eels), drag anchor.
  - Three decisions for Albert, each with a recommendation, and a build order.

No change to the game this block.
