| Moment | Sound |
| --- | --- |

| Jellyfish sting / shut valve | an electric zap |
| Crash | a metallic clank |

| Valve closing | a wet groan |

| End of transmission | the burst: a whine into static |
| Robbie talking | a few blips per subtitle, pitched by the words |
| Buttons, Launch | clicks and a clunk |

Event sounds play when the playhead passes them at normal or double speed; scrubbing and fast-forward stay
quiet. M or the speaker button mutes. Browsers only allow sound after a first click.

Checked by recording a replay with Godot's movie writer and measuring the audio: peaks at 0.0 s (launch),
8.9 and 17.4 s (valve closing), 11.8 and 15.0 s (the two lurkers), 19.2 s (exit), ambience around -28 dBFS.
