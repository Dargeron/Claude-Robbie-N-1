# Session 39 · 2026-10-26 · Back to the flat desk: speed, lamps, no lid, Bathy swims

Albert played 0.3.0. The 3D view "feels clunky and hard to use": keep upgrading the 2D desk. The machine is "not
steampunky enough", "too shiny", the red handles "too clean and too round" (a glossy red bubble on every lever); the
scope is wonderful. He asked for five concept styles for the handles, and for these details:

1. **Speed.** Picking speed 1 still played reruns at 4: on every dispatch the console set the replay to skip at 8x
   to just before where the previous Robbie was lost, whatever the knob said. Now the knob has ½ / 1 / 2 / AUTO (the
   old 1, 2, 4 and AUTO), starts on 1, and every dive plays at what it says; the skip-ahead is gone. AUTO plays quiet
   water at the new 2 and slows to the new 1 around events (`ReplayPlayer.AUTO_SLOW` 2.0). The scope's readout shows
   speeds as the knob marks them (`WireRecorder.marked`).
2. **No magic text** (now a rule in CLAUDE.md). `Desk.draw_lamp` (a lamp with a fixed label) and
   `Desk.draw_odometer` (number wheels, factored out of the debt counter). Dispatch: SAFE and READY lamps, the serial
   on a three-wheel counter. Sea trials: "16 OF 20 TO PASS" and BEACON / CERTIFIED lamps. Hulls lost on two red
   wheels. LIVE lamp (no more LIVE/HELD), "THE WALL", "HULL IN THE WATER", the PUT BACK HIS DECK pull always shown
   with a lamp, no NEW stamped on the drums (a glint instead).
3. **The glass lid is gone**: from the card table, the console, the notes and regulations, the layout, the plate and
   the sprites. Cards go straight into the slots.
4. **Bathy's flair** came from the old station's dive view (smooth heading, paddling fins, bubbles); the scope's glyph
   snapped to 16 headings and glided. Now: a sub-pixel hover, a sway while swimming, a heading that eases after the
   real one, pods paddling, a faint wake. View only. `game/tools/hover_clip.tscn` records it.
5. **2D only**: `operator.gd` opens flat and builds no 3D unless `ROBBIE_VIEW=chair`; the switch and V key are gone.
6. **Handle styles**: `tools/art/handle_styles.py`, five sheets of the same six parts (dispatch lever, PRINT pull,
   wheel, knob, toggle, key): Foundry, Admiralty, Telegraph, Brass & leather, Jury-rigged. In docs/art/handles/.

The plate was re-rendered (grimy, weathered) without the lid's seat, hinge and latch.
