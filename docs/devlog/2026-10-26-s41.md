# Session 41 · 2026-10-26 · The pull chain, the press prints

Albert played 0.5.0 ("we're getting there") with nine notes:

1. **No laser in the Jellyfish.** The console had always issued FIRE LASER with shift 3 (the Nursery); the old
   station let you use it on the Jellyfish, and `jellyfish/intended_laser.rbt` still certifies there. Moved the
   `shoot` plate to shift 2, reworded both memos and Op. 5's jelly note.
2. **Printing.** `CardTable.add_printed` no longer slides the card into the tray: it feeds out of the slot in five
   jerks (`FEED_TIME`, a `chuff` each), drawn inside a clipping Control (`_mouth`) so what hasn't come out is still
   inside the press, then falls onto the platter with gravity and a bounce. Cards in the feed count as on the tray.
3. **PRINT's plate on top** (layout). 4. **A heavier PRINT lever**: a code-drawn bronze arm from the slot to a bigger
   scalloped pull; a wider slot on a bronze track.
5. **Only the wheels choose**: the drums ignore touches.
6. **The value drum has a small wheel** (`value_knob`, sprite `wheel_value`; `build_wheel` scales its proportions).
   The CONDITION wheel and plate moved to make room.
7. **The pull chain** (`PullChain` in console/dispatch.gd): 22 links, Verlet with 14 constraint passes per substep,
   a heavy handle, a pull rod on a damped spring out of the ceiling. Pulled past `fire` it dispatches, with a steam
   whistle (new synthesised `whistle`, `chain`, `chuff` in tools/gen_audio.py) and steam off the whistle baked in the
   plate. If the deck can't go, the rod stops short with a clunk and HOLD is lit. The lever, cover, ARMED, their
   sprites and the "cover" refusal are gone.
8. **Shadows that turned with the parts** (and were cut square at the sprite's edge): the sprites are rendered with
   no shadow catcher, and `Desk.draw_sprite` draws the part's own silhouette, dark and offset towards the lower right,
   before the part. Recorder keys too.
9. **Both dials take taps and drags**: the speed knob drags to the nearest mark; a tap on the sector knob clicks to
   the next open sector.

The play-through gained checks for the speed drag, the sector tap, and the drum glass ignoring touches.
