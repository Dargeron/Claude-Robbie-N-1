# 0.6.0-chain · The pull chain, and the press prints

Your notes from 0.5.0, built in:

- **The laser is back for the Jellyfish.** FIRE LASER is now issued with the Jellyfish shift (it used to arrive one
  shift later), so you can burn your way through, or go dark: your choice. Op. 5 has an opinion.
- **Dispatch is a pull chain.** It hangs from the ceiling with a red T-handle. Grab the handle and pull it all the way
  down: the steam whistle blows and Robbie drops. Let go and it springs back up, jumping and swinging on its links. If
  the deck can't go, it stops short with a clunk and the **HOLD** lamp is lit; **READY** means it will go. The small
  lever, the red cover and the ARMED plate are gone.
- **The press really prints.** Pull PRINT and the card comes out of the slot a notch at a time, chuk-chuk-chuk,
  already printed, then drops onto the platter below with a little bounce. Pick it up from there.
- **PRINT** has its plate at the top now, and the lever is a heavy bronze arm with a bigger red pull.
- **Only the wheels choose.** The drums are behind glass: touching them does nothing. The value drum (distances,
  charge, card number) has its own small wheel now.
- **Shadows stay put.** The parts no longer carry their shadow round with them when they turn; no more square shadows.
- **Both dials take a tap and a drag.** The recorder's speed knob: tap to click it to the next mark, or drag it round.
  The sector selector: drag it, tap a number, or tap the knob to click on to the next open sector.

Install as before: the `.apk` on the tablet (it updates the copy you have), or unzip the Windows build and run
`RobbieN1.exe`. Your progress is kept.
