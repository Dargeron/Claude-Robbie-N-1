# 0.4.0-desk · Back to the flat desk, and your details

The 3D view is set aside; the game is the flat desk again, and we keep improving it.

Your playtest notes, built in:

- **Speed.** The recorder's knob now has four marks: **½, 1, 2 and AUTO**. They are the old 1, 2 and 4, plus AUTO.
  It starts on **1**, and every dive, including every rerun, plays at what the knob says. Nothing speeds up by
  itself any more: the old skip-ahead on reruns is gone. **AUTO** runs quiet water at 2 and slows to 1 for anything
  that happens. (The arrow keys turn the knob too.)
- **No more words changing by themselves on the machine.** Where a label used to swap its text, there are now lamps
  with fixed painted names, and numbers that turn on little number wheels:
  - Dispatch: a **SAFE** lamp (cover down) and a **READY** lamp (cover up, deck fine). The next Robbie's number
    turns on a counter in the plate.
  - Sea trials: "16 OF 20 TO PASS" painted under the lamps, and **BEACON** and **CERTIFIED** lamps.
  - The Company's counter: the hulls lost turn on two small red wheels.
  - The scope's **LIVE** lamp just lights up; the Wall and Bathy's porthole have fixed name plates; the brass pull
    that puts back the deck of the Robbie you're watching is always there, with a lamp that lights when it would do
    something.
- **The glass lid is gone.** Drag cards straight from the tray into the slots.
- **Bathy swims again:** on the scope he hovers a little, sways as he paddles, turns smoothly, and leaves a faint
  wake. Only the drawing; his course is the same as ever.

Install as before: the `.apk` on the tablet (it updates the copy you have), or unzip the Windows build and run
`RobbieN1.exe`. Your progress is kept.

Separately: five concept sheets of handle styles for the machine, waiting for your pick.
