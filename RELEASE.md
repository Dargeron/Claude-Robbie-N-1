# 0.5.0-admiralty · The Admiralty desk

Your pick, style 2, on the whole machine: ship's bronze gone dark, panels in navy grey-green enamel, and everything
your hand works in valve red, matte and chipped down to the bronze. No glossy red balls any more:

- The two selector wheels are valve handwheels, their rims knuckled where your fingers go.
- The dispatch lever ends in a red **T-handle**; **PRINT** is a scalloped red pull, like a sea-cock.
- The speed and sector knobs are **wing knobs**, the pointed end showing where they point.
- The NOT switch has a red spoon on its end; the recorder keys are red buttons in bronze bezels.
- The dispatch cover, the crank's grip, the capsule's bands, the reels' hubs: the same chipped valve red.
- Name plates are bronze rubbed bright so they stay readable; lamps sit in bronze bezels.

Everything from 0.4.0 is still there (speed knob ½ / 1 / 2 / AUTO, lamps instead of changing words, no lid, Bathy
swimming).

Install as before: the `.apk` on the tablet (it updates the copy you have), or unzip the Windows build and run
`RobbieN1.exe`. Your progress is kept.
