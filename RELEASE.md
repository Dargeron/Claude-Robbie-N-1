# 0.3.0-views · Two ways to sit at the desk

**This build plays the same game two ways, so you can pick.** The little switch at the top left (or the V key)
flips between them while you play, and the game remembers your choice:

- **A · 3D:** you sit at the console in its room. The lower machines slope towards you like a desk, a lamp sways
  over it, papers and a cold mug lie in front of you, dust drifts in the light. **Pinch** (or the mouse wheel) to lean
  in towards a machine; pinch out to sit back. Memos and the binder come up flat in front of you to read.
- **B · FLAT:** the console face on, filling the screen, as before.

Both now use the lived-in desk: forty years of rust, grime, mismatched panels, cables and a pipe run.

Your playtest notes, built in:

- **Red means hands.** Everything you can turn, pull, press or drag has red on it: the wheel rims and grips, the
  knobs, the recorder keys, the lever handles, the lid's handle, the NOT switch, the capsule, a band along every card.
  Op. 5 says so in his first note.
- **The drums are back.** Turning the ORDER and CONDITION wheels rolls printed drums behind the glass above them, so
  you see the choice between the marks *and* the ones before and after. You can also roll a drum with your finger.
- **Op. 5's notes, written on the machine.** No more post-its: he writes in grease pencil on the glass of the reader,
  the scope and the press, loops the thing he means and draws an arrow to it. When a note has done its job it's
  wiped off with a thumb. They never get in the way of a touch.
- **Pulping a card:** it gets crumpled into a ball, tossed into the chute, and chewed; the shreds spit back out.

Install as before: the `.apk` on the tablet (it updates the copy you have), or unzip the Windows build and run
`RobbieN1.exe`. Your progress is kept.
